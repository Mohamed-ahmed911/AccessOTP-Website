ABOUT ACCESSOTP

Security and accessibility.
Working together.

We developed two Android Studio applications to demonstrate OTP verification without requiring a visually impaired user to read or manually enter a one-time code.

WHO WE ARE

Making the next step easier.

AccessOTP is a student-built proof of concept featuring a fictional bank app and a same-device Authenticator written in Kotlin. The goal is to reduce reliance on SMS reading, code memorization or assistance during sign-in.

We are not offering a real banking authentication service. The Android implementation demonstrates a protected inter-app exchange in a local testing environment.

✔ Two Android applications
✔ Automatic OTP handling after a correct demo PIN
✔ Accessibility-focused PIN interaction
✔ No real bank accounts or transfers
OUR MISSION

More independent digital access

Explore an authentication workflow that reduces barriers for blind and visually impaired users while preserving the intention of a second verification step.

OUR VISION

Inclusive banking technology

Support future accessible authentication methods that institutions can evaluate, integrate and independently secure.

OBJECTIVES

What we set out to achieve

Reduce manual OTP steps

Automate handling of the one-time code inside the Android demonstration.

Improve PIN accessibility

Offer vibration choices and a hidden-number keypad for users who benefit from haptic input.

Demonstrate a safe boundary

Use fictional bank data and a companion app, and clearly distinguish prototype controls from production banking security.
