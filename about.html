OUR STORY

Technology that puts people first.

AccessOTP is an accessibility-first product concept exploring a simpler banking authentication experience for people with visual impairments.

WHO WE ARE

Making secure access more inclusive.

We are a team developing a proof-of-concept that demonstrates how authorized banking systems could communicate with an accessibility-focused companion app to remove manual OTP entry.

The current demonstration uses fictional banking software. It is not connected to an actual financial institution.

Our Mission

To explore an accessible, permission-based verification experience that minimizes barriers for people with visual impairments while respecting digital security.

Our Vision

A digital banking experience where assistive technology and secure authentication work together so more people can access essential services independently.

WHAT DRIVES US

Our objectives

Reduce entry barriers

Demonstrate verification without requiring users to visually read or type a code.

Model authorized integration

Explain the need for a secure, bank-approved request and response workflow.

Build a testable prototype

Show the full concept using a fictional bank and a simulated AccessOTP service.
