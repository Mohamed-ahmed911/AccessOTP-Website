HONEST PROTOTYPE BOUNDARY

What is actually protected?

Android implementation: The apps communicate using a signature-protected service. The bank creates a random OTP and fresh challenge, encrypts the payload to the Authenticator's Android Keystore RSA key, and checks the returned HMAC-SHA256 proof. The Authenticator checks the challenge age and recent in-memory nonce reuse. Browser simulation: shows these steps visually only, without implementing bank security. This is not a real bank service, full trusted-device enrollment, hardware attestation or a server-side second factor.
